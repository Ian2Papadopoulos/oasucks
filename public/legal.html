Terms of use

1. What this is — and what it isn't

OASUCKS is a free, unofficial, non-commercial app that shows live bus and trolley arrivals for Athens, plus flags submitted by other riders.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to OASA, OSY, STASY, or any other transport operator or public authority. The name is a joke made by a passenger. Any trade marks mentioned belong to their owners and are used only to say which service the data describes.

2. The data is third-party, best-effort, and often wrong

Arrival times, vehicle positions, stop locations and route information come from OASA's telematics service. We pass them through; we do not produce, verify or control them.

Do not rely on this app for anything that matters. Arrival estimates are OASA's own predictions and are regularly wrong by minutes. Buses go missing from the feed. Vehicle positions are typically 30–60 seconds stale. If you have a flight, an exam or an interview, leave early and check the operator's own information.

3. Rider reports

  • Reports are unverified. They are one person's claim, filed in a hurry, from a phone, about a moving vehicle. A flag being absent means nothing at all.
  • A report marked “unconfirmed” has not been corroborated by anyone and may well be mistaken. It is shown faint for exactly that reason.
  • Reports expire on their own — between 5 minutes and 2 hours depending on type — and are then deleted.
  • You are responsible for what you file.

4. What you must not do

  • File reports you know to be untrue.
  • Automate reporting, or use more than one device or identity to make a report look corroborated when it isn't.
  • Use the service to identify, follow, harass or target any individual. The app deliberately has no free-text field, no photos and no way to name a person — do not try to work around that.
  • Use the service to avoid paying fares. Always carry a valid ticket. Reports exist so riders know the state of the service they are paying for.
  • Scrape, overload or attempt to break the service, or use it to place load on OASA's systems.

We may remove any report and block any device that breaks these rules, without notice.

5. Reporting a problem (notice and action)

If a report is abusive, false, or targets a person, tell us at CONTACT@EXAMPLE.COM with enough detail to find it (line/station, issue type, roughly when you saw it). This is our contact point and notice-and-action mechanism under the Digital Services Act (Regulation 2022/2065); we are a micro-scale hosting service and rely on the liability exemption in Art. 6.

You can also knock a report down inside the app: tap “Not there” on it. Two independent riders doing that removes it for everyone.

Privacy policy

No accounts. No sign-up. No advertising. No analytics. The controller under the GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) is the operator of this instance, at CONTACT@EXAMPLE.COM.

2. What is not processed

  • No account, name, email or phone number.
  • No free-text field anywhere in a report. You choose from a fixed menu. There is no way to type a comment, upload a photo, or name a person — by design.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts. Map code (Leaflet) is served from this app's own domain, not a CDN.
  • No cookies. localStorage holds only your language, pinned stops and anonymous id — strictly necessary storage under the ePrivacy Directive, which is why there is no consent banner.
  • No location history. Your position answers the request in front of it and is then discarded. The app cannot reconstruct where you have been.

3. Who else sees your data

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosts the app and its storage as our processor (Art. 28), possibly outside the EEA under Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • OASA (telematics.oasa.gr) — source of all arrival data. Requests are made by our server, so OASA sees our address, not yours.
  • OpenStreetMap / CARTO — map tiles are fetched by your browser, so they see your IP address and the area you are viewing.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.

5. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time — revoke location permission, or turn off notifications.

Because the service holds no identifying data about you, we usually cannot find “your” records from an email alone. That is a deliberate design outcome, not an evasion. In practice: to erase a report, open the app, find it in the report list and tap ✕ — only you can. To erase everything else, clear the site's data in your browser; your anonymous id goes with it and any remaining reports expire on their own within hours.

You may complain to a supervisory authority — in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, dpa.gr.

6. Children, security, changes

The app is not directed at children and collects no age information. All traffic is HTTPS; reports carry no identity beyond a random token, withdrawal is checked server-side against that token, and reporter tokens are never sent to other users' devices. Material changes are noted by the date at the top of this page.
